Mimac vs Luna Display

These two are often searched together and rarely compared fairly, because they are different things. Luna Display is a hardware dongle that turns an iPad into a display for a Mac you are sitting at. Mimac is a remote desktop that turns an iPad Pro into the display and input device for a Mac anywhere.

Where Luna Display is a better choice

Pick Luna if you want your iPad as a second display next to a Mac you are already using, including over a cable, with the Mac's own keyboard and mouse doing the work. It is hardware, so it is independent of any subscription, and it works with Intel Macs. If the Mac is always within arm's reach, a display is the simpler tool.

Where Mimac is a better choice

Pick Mimac if the iPad Pro is meant to be the only display, keyboard and trackpad for the Mac, especially a Mac mini or Mac Studio in another room or another city. Nothing plugs into the Mac. The iPad's Magic Keyboard drives macOS with real gestures and every shortcut, audio flows both ways, and a dragged rectangle lands on the Mac's clipboard. It also costs less than the dongle.

Bottom line

Second display: Luna. Remote desktop: Mimac. Some people will own both, and that is fine.
