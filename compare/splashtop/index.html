Where Splashtop Personal is a better choice

Pick Splashtop if free on your own network is the whole requirement, and a Mac with a monitor attached is fine. Pick it if you want remote access through a vendor relay rather than setting up Tailscale, and $23.99 a year suits you. It also works with Intel Macs and across many platforms, which Mimac does not attempt.

Where Mimac is a better choice

Pick Mimac for a headless Mac mini or Mac Studio: Splashtop needs the Mac to have a display, Mimac creates one at your iPad's exact resolution. Pick it for the feel: 120 fps on your network, trackpad gestures that behave like a MacBook, lossless audio, your microphone on the Mac, and a screenshot gesture that lands on the Mac's clipboard. Pick it if you would rather nothing of anyone's sit between your iPad and your Mac.

Bottom line

Splashtop is a fine free answer to "can I see my Mac from the couch". Mimac is the answer to "can I work from my iPad Pro as if I were at the Mac". Try both; the trial costs nothing either way.
